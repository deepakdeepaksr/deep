## 2. Schematic organisation (performed 2026-10-27)

**Goal:** professional, readable arrangement of the 6L schematic without touching a single electrical connection.

**What was done** — rigid *block re-packing*, verified netlist-identical:
1. Every drawn subcircuit (symbols + their wires + their labels, joined by real pin/wire coincidence) was treated as one rigid unit — 301 units, merged to **39 blocks** by a 12 mm drawn-proximity rule (model-free, so no connection can be split).
2. Blocks classified into **functional bands** and shelf-packed left→right, top→bottom, aligned to the 1.27 mm grid with uniform 16/9 mm gutters, inside the A0 sheet and clear of the title block.
3. Dashed **band outlines + bold titles** added: `1. POWER INPUT, PROTECTION AND REGULATION` · `3. MCU HOST INTERFACE` · `5. I2C BUS AND MULTIPLEXERS` · `6. SENSOR FRONT ENDS` · `7. LORA RADIO AND SERIAL LINK` · `9. TEST POINTS AND MISC`.
4. **545 stray field texts** (hidden Footprint/Datasheet/Description parked at 0,0 far from their symbol) snapped back onto their symbol anchor — this was the main source of "messy" selection boxes and off-sheet junk.
5. The **14 simulation-probe notes** (`!padne …`) kept functional and moved into a titled `SIMULATION PROBES` column at x ≈ 1029 mm.

**Geometry outcome:** content now spans x 20.3 → 1005.8 mm, y 38.1 → 690.9 mm (was an uneven blob reaching x 965 / y 444 with everything parked against the left edge); 0 coordinates off-sheet; 0 stray field texts; 71.4 % of drawing coordinates on the 1.27 mm grid.
