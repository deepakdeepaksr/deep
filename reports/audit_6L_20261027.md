## Fix list, worst first
1. **Vias-in-pad untented (56)** + paste over them (82) — tent/fill, or move the via off the pad.
2. **No MPN/Manufacturer in the BOM** — add fields (and fix schematic annotation) before ordering.
3. **Silkscreen**: 124 DRC warnings, 6 features inside mask openings, 0.12 mm web text (want ≥0.15 mm).
4. **Acute copper**: 12 wedges ≤90° (min 11.3°) and 45 overlapping track ends — add teardrops / re-miter.
5. **Annular 0.10 mm** on the smallest vias — raise to ≥0.15 mm for standard fab relief.
6. Plane edge clearance is 0.5005 mm against a 0.5 mm rule — widen to 0.6 mm for drill-registration margin.
7. Housekeeping: F1/SW1 pin-count, SOT-23 library mismatch, H1–H4 footprints absent from schematic, 24 corner copper islands, add stackup + (optional) 50 Ω widths, add panel fiducials.

All 19 checks are reproducible: `_audit6l/t1_board.py` (board geometry), `_audit6l/t2_fabfiles.py` (production files), `_audit6l/t3_reports.py` (ERC/DRC/parity/CPL/BOM/impedance).
